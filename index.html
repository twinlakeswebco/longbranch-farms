We know every animal by name.

Long Branch Farms started with a simple conviction: people in our community deserve to know exactly what they're eating and how it was raised. We're two working parents with our little boy.

Our pigs have access to pasture and woods. We mix their feed by hand every day and supplement with milk from our A2/A2 dairy cow. Our beef is finished on grass. It's the same food we put on our own table.
